About Unity Space

OUR STORY

Turning curiosity into aerospace.

Unity Space is a student-led aerospace club where people from different disciplines come together to learn, build and test real systems.

Our mission is to create an environment where engineering, creativity and teamwork can take ideas from a sketchbook to the sky.

We believe the strongest aerospace teams are built not only with technical skills, but with curiosity, responsibility and a willingness to learn from every launch.
